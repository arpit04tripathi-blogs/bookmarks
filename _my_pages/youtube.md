## Video Resolution

Here are the most common screen resolutions, their pixel counts, and standard aspect ratios:

| Common Name               | Resolution (Pixels)   | Aspect Ratio |
|---                        |---                    |---            |
| SD (Standard Definition)  | 640 x 480             | 4:3           |
| HD (720p)                 | 1280 x 720            | 16:9          |
| FHD (Full HD / 1080p)     | 1920 x 1080           | 16:9          |
| QHD (2K / 1440p)          | 2560 x 1440           | 16:9          |
| UW-QHD (Ultra-Wide)       | 3440 x 1440           | 21:9          |
| 4K UHD (2160p)            | 3840 x 2160           | 16:9          |
| 8K UHD (4320p)            | 7680 x 4320           | 16:9          |

- 30 fps : cinematic and natural
- 60 fps : smooth (gaming or B-roll), slow motions
